# Gap Assessment Methodology

## Phase 1: Scope and context

Define:

- Care service type: domiciliary care, care home, supported living, nursing care, children’s service, agency care.
- Systems in scope: digital care records, HR/payroll, email, phones, laptops, CCTV, medication system, finance, cloud storage.
- Data subjects: residents/service users, families, staff, volunteers, visitors, suppliers.
- Data types: identity, contact, care plans, medication, safeguarding, health, financial, employment, DBS, incident records.
- Legal context: UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, common law duty of confidentiality where relevant, CQC governance expectations, NHS DSPT where applicable.

## Phase 2: Evidence collection

Request evidence before interviewing stakeholders. See `templates/evidence-request-list.md`.

Evidence examples:

- Data protection policy
- Privacy notices
- Record of processing activities
- DPIA register
- Supplier contracts
- Data sharing agreements
- Breach log
- Subject access request log
- Training records
- Asset register
- Vulnerability reports
- Backup reports
- Access review records
- Retention schedule

## Phase 3: Control assessment

For each control:

1. Review policy/process evidence.
2. Interview the process owner.
3. Check system or operational evidence.
4. Rate maturity from 0 to 4.
5. Record the gap.
6. Record risk likelihood and impact.
7. Recommend remediation.
8. Assign owner and target date.

## Phase 4: Risk scoring

Risk score = Likelihood x Impact.

Likelihood:

| Score | Description |
|------:|-------------|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Almost certain |

Impact:

| Score | Description |
|------:|-------------|
| 1 | Minor process issue |
| 2 | Limited operational impact |
| 3 | Moderate compliance/security impact |
| 4 | Major regulatory, service-user or business impact |
| 5 | Severe harm, reportable breach, CQC/regulatory concern |

## Phase 5: Reporting

Produce:

- Executive summary
- Heatmap of top risks
- Control maturity summary
- High-risk findings
- Quick wins
- 30/60/90-day remediation plan
- Residual risk and risk acceptance items

## Phase 6: Follow-up and assurance

Track remediation using:

- Risk register
- Evidence register
- Monthly compliance review
- Vulnerability remediation SLA tracking
- Policy review cycle
- DPIA and supplier review calendar
