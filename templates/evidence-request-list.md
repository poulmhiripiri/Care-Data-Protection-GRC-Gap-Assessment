# Evidence Request List

## Governance

- Data protection policy
- Information governance policy
- Data protection lead/DPO appointment evidence
- Management meeting minutes showing risk/compliance oversight
- Data protection training records
- Policy register and review dates

## Data inventory and privacy

- Record of Processing Activities
- Data flow diagrams
- Privacy notices for service users, families, staff and website visitors
- CCTV privacy notice/signage if applicable
- Consent forms where used

## DPIA and data sharing

- DPIA register
- Completed DPIAs
- DPIA screening forms
- Data sharing agreements
- Data sharing map
- Supplier contracts and processor agreements

## Security and vulnerability management

- Asset register
- MFA reports
- Access review records
- Backup reports
- Device encryption reports
- Endpoint protection dashboard
- Vulnerability scan reports
- Patch management reports
- Firewall/security configuration review evidence

## Incidents and individual rights

- Breach log
- Incident response procedure
- ICO reporting decision records
- Subject access request log
- Complaint log
- Lessons learned reports

## Retention and disposal

- Retention schedule
- Archive records
- Shredding certificates
- Digital deletion evidence
