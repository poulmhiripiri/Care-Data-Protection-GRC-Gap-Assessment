# Maturity Scoring Model

## Scoring scale

| Score | Rating | Description | Evidence expectation |
|------:|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| 0 | Not in place | No control exists | No document, no owner, no consistent practice |
| 1 | Initial | Informal or reactive | Some activity, but inconsistent and not evidenced |
| 2 | Defined | Documented but not embedded | Policy/process exists but limited operational evidence |
| 3 | Managed | Implemented and evidenced | Evidence shows process is followed and reviewed |
| 4 | Optimised | Continuously improved | Metrics, trend analysis, governance reporting and improvements |

## Risk scoring

Risk score = Likelihood x Impact.

| Risk score | Priority | Example response |
|-----------:|----------|------------------|
| 1-4 | Low | Monitor through routine review |
| 5-9 | Medium | Assign owner and target date |
| 10-15 | High | Prioritise remediation within 30-60 days |
| 16-25 | Critical | Immediate management attention and urgent mitigation |

## Residual risk

After remediation, reassess:

- Has likelihood reduced?
- Has impact reduced?
- Is evidence available?
- Is the remaining risk accepted by the right owner?
- Does the risk need to be escalated?
