# Sample Gap Assessment Summary

## Organisation profile

A medium-sized domiciliary care provider with 120 staff, 300 service users, a digital care planning platform, Microsoft 365 email, outsourced payroll, mobile care worker devices and regular data sharing with local authorities, GPs, pharmacies and emergency services.

## Key findings

### High risk 1: No complete record of processing activities

The organisation could not evidence a complete data inventory showing what personal data is processed, the lawful basis, special category condition, retention period and sharing partners.

**Impact:** Weak accountability, unclear data flows and poor ability to respond to rights requests or incidents.

**Recommendation:** Build a ROPA covering service users, staff, suppliers, CCTV, care planning, payroll and safeguarding processes.

### High risk 2: DPIA screening not embedded

New systems are procured without formal privacy screening. CCTV and digital care record changes have not been assessed for high-risk processing.

**Impact:** Privacy risks may be identified too late and mitigations may be missing.

**Recommendation:** Introduce DPIA screening into procurement and project approval.

### High risk 3: Supplier contracts not consistently reviewed

Several suppliers process care records or staff data, but contracts and processor clauses are not centrally recorded.

**Impact:** Third-party processing risk and weak assurance over security, deletion and breach notification.

**Recommendation:** Build supplier register, review processor agreements and perform due diligence on high-risk suppliers.

### High risk 4: Vulnerability management not formalised

The organisation has endpoint protection but no documented vulnerability scanning cadence, patch SLA or risk acceptance process.

**Impact:** Increased chance of compromise affecting special category data.

**Recommendation:** Create asset inventory, define vulnerability scanning cadence and track remediation in a risk register.

## Quick wins

- Enable MFA for all cloud accounts.
- Create breach log and incident decision form.
- Update privacy notice for service users and families.
- Build data sharing map.
- Start quarterly access reviews.

## Management message

The organisation has several good operational practices but lacks consistent evidence and formal governance. The recommended approach is to prioritise accountability, DPIA screening, supplier assurance, access control and vulnerability governance over the next 90 days.
